The groups describe proportions and build, the same for both sexes: `chibi`, `compact`, `slim`, `long`,
`balanced`, `rounded`. They stylise real people's statistics at different ages and stand for no
particular character.

The test bodies (`sotaigen/data/bodies/<sex>/test/`) are only for this tool's tests: neither their names
nor their parameters are contract.
