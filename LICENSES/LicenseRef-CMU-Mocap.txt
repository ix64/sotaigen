Motion clips in motions/clips/ are reduced from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database,
http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/

The database's home page states that the dataset of motions is free for all uses.

Acknowledgment requested by the site: the data used in this project was obtained from
mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

Which source files each clip comes from, and how it was reduced, is in motions/SOURCES.md.
